Keys

Octave buttons

Both octave buttons show the color of the current octave; they're dark at the lowest and highest octave in that direction.

Pitch bend and pressure

Keys you hold can change color as you bend and press them. A bend moves from the center color toward the down or up color, all the way at the key's full travel; pressure mixes from no pressure to full. With both, pressure sets the color and a bend moves it. Keys lit by incoming notes keep their own colors.
